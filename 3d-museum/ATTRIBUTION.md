# Attribution

This project combines original source code with third-party venue and artifact
assets. The project code is released under the MIT License, but imported 3D
assets remain under their original licenses.

## Venue

- OpenVGAL, Open Virtual Gallery
- Source: https://github.com/lbartworks/openvgal
- License: MIT License
- Local license copy: `public/venue/openvgal/LICENSE`
- Included assets: selected Minimalist GLB venue templates and `catalog.json`

Artifact model credits are also stored per exhibit in `src/worldArtifacts.ts`.

- Smithsonian Open Access, National Museum of Asian Art, CC0: Chinese bronze,
  Buddhist, stone cave, porcelain, and related artifacts.
- Minneapolis Institute of Art, CC0: selected bronze and Buddhist artifacts.
- Thomas Flynn, Museum fur Kunst und Gewerbe Hamburg, CC BY: Water-Moon
  Guanyin model.
- Virtual Museums of Malopolska / National Museum in Krakow, CC0: Ding-form
  censer.
- Sketchfab user `33961810`, CC BY: Sanxingdui bronze mask model.

  porcelain, and Shang elephant-form vessel.
- Fraunhofer IGD CultLab3D via three.js examples, CC BY-NC: Nefertiti bust.
- DailyArt via three.js examples, CC BY: Venetian carnival mask.
- Deutsches Schifffahrtsmuseum open data, MIT: marine chronometer and naval
  river mine.
- Smithsonian National Air and Space Museum via Google model-viewer,
  CC BY-NC-SA: Apollo 11 spacesuit.
- Smithsonian Open Access / 3d.si.edu, CC0: cuneiform tablet, Greek Slave,
  Marco Cat, Tree of Life, Junkanoo headdress, fossils, shells, plants,
  orchids, and related natural-history artifacts.
- Loic Norgeot via Khronos glTF sample models, CC BY 4.0: mosquito in amber.

- Khronos glTF sample models, open-source sample assets: antique camera,

## Publishing Notes

Some imported assets use non-commercial licenses such as CC BY-NC and
CC BY-NC-SA. Keep those license terms visible when publishing this repository,
and review them before using the complete asset bundle in commercial products.
